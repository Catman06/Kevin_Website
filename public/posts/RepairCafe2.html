At the Peoria Repair Cafe on May 19, 2026, I began offering to install Linux onto people's computers in addition to repairing the computers.

In preparation, I took 4 computers that were no longer wanted from work and installed Zorin, Mint, Ubuntu, and Kubuntu on them so people could try using different Distros/DEs. I then wrote up some info on what Linux is and why someone would want to use it, what a desktop environment is, and descriptions of the 4 distros and DEs I had on the example laptops. The info was printed out, and the stuff on Linux itself was turned into a penguin themed brochure.

The reception to the offering of Linux and my information about it was quite positive. Several people told me that the information I provided was helpful. One of the other volunteers, who was having issues with their laptop, even decided to try out Zorin and another person brought their desktop that they had installed Ubuntu onto to have Windows removed.

For the next repair cafe I intend to continue to offer Linux help. I am additionally considering creating an email to allow people to email me with Linux support questions, but I don't know if I want to commit to providing a service like that. I might just make them either figure it out themselves (they have the internet, and it is the more Linux-y way) or wait for the next repair cafe.

The repair cafe itself is also to be featured on WTVP's "You Gotta See This" on May 21, 2026, though I don't believe that my table was captured at all.
